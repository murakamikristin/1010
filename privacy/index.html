Privacy Support

Effective May 30, 2026

Privacy Policy

TenTen does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

Data Collection

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, account systems, or external databases controlled by TenTen.

Local Game Data

Game progress, high scores, themes, settings, and other preferences may be stored locally on your device so the app can work as expected. This data is not sent to TenTen.

In-App Purchases

TenTen offers optional in-app purchases through Apple In-App Purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple. TenTen does not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID password.

The app may receive purchase entitlement information from Apple to unlock purchased cosmetic features or confirm optional tip purchases.

Children's Privacy

TenTen does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact murakamikristin@g.ucla.edu.
